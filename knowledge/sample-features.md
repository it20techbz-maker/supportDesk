## Export the daily settlement report to Excel

Open Reports, choose Daily Settlement, select the date, then choose Export and pick Excel. The file is saved to your downloads folder. Reports are available for the last 90 days.

## Change the schedule of a cron job

Cron job schedules are changed by our support team on request. Please send the job ID, the environment (PROD or UAT) and the new run time, and we will raise a change ticket for you.
